Coches Benejúzar / Services

Everything a convertible roof can need.

Four workshop disciplines under one roof.

Fabric & covers

Soft top repair and replacement

Most roofs arrive with the same story.

  • Original-pattern fabric, vinyl or hard top covers
  • Rear window replacement, including heated glass
  • Seam, corner and tension repairs
  • Seal renewal and a full water test on handover
Mechanism

Hydraulics: cylinders, pumps, hoses

A roof that stops halfway is almost always hydraulic.

  • Leaking, seized or scored cylinders
  • Pump, reservoir and fluid service
  • Hose and fitting replacement
  • Linkage alignment and sequence reset
Electronics

Electrical diagnostics and control units

Convertibles refuse to move for safety reasons more often than for mechanical ones.

  • Fault code reading and live testing
  • Microswitches, latch and position sensors
  • Motor, relay and control unit repair
  • Wiring loom repair at the folding points
Interior

Cabrio upholstery and restoration

Sun and water age a convertible interior faster than any other car.

  • Headlining and interior trim
  • Seat and panel re-covering in leather or fabric
  • Tonneau covers and boot linings
  • Carpet drying and odour treatment after leaks

Frequently asked

Yes. Retractable hard tops share the same hydraulics and sensors.

Can you come to the car?

Diagnosis and fitting need the workshop.
